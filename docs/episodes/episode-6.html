Summary

This episode follows Rico through a Vegas run where the cards seem to bend in his favor one night and turn against him the next. From a powerful heater at the Bellagio to a cold streak at the Venetian, the experience teaches a timeless lesson: poker is driven by rhythm, variance, and the discipline to separate luck from skill.

The story centers on the idea that a player can create their own luck by making correct decisions, reading patterns, and respecting the flow of the game. Through a mix of hot hands, bad beats, and strategic reflection, Rico explains the power of the Fundamental Theorem of Poker and the importance of “losing the least, winning the most.” When the rhythm turns, the disciplined player adapts instead of forcing action.
